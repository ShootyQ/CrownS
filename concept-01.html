Section 01 Observed Evidence

Observed Evidence

The work starts where small clues become expensive problems.

Inspector note

Every inspection starts with visible evidence. Roof wear, drainage patterns, cracked sealants, movement around openings, and deferred maintenance usually speak before the seller ever does. Rex documents what he sees and explains how each issue should be understood.

  • Roof condition is reviewed for age, wear, flashing details, and visible damage.
  • Drainage and grading are noted before moisture turns into interior problems.
  • Visible wear often points to repair cost that should be considered early.
Section 02 Inspection Categories

Scope Of Review

What gets checked on every inspection.

Category What Rex Looks For Why It Matters
Site / Drainage

Grading, runoff behavior, and where moisture may be collecting.

Water almost always wins if the site is working against the house.

Roof / Attic

Surface wear, penetrations, ventilation clues, and visible aging patterns.

Upper-envelope issues can multiply into insulation, moisture, and structural problems.

Structure / Movement

Settlement signs, cracking patterns, and load-related concerns.

The difference between cosmetic and consequential matters to decisions and negotiation.

Systems / Interior

Visible HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and finish-condition indicators.

Performance issues shape both immediate repairs and future ownership cost.
