Approach

A home reveals itself in the details.

01

Architecture first

This direction treats the site like an architectural presentation rather than a contractor flyer. The layout is sparse, the imagery leads, and the message stays disciplined.

02

Quiet authority

Everything is meant to feel composed and intentional, so the trust comes from the tone and the work, not from overexplaining.

03

Inspected, not sold

The story centers on observation: rooflines, drainage, systems, structure, materials, and the kind of judgment buyers cannot get from listing photos.

Craft

The work should feel deliberate before the report is ever opened.

Concept 09 uses the working image as proof of seriousness. Rex is not presented as a mascot or a generic smiling headshot. He is shown doing the actual work, in the conditions where clients need judgment most.

Roofline

Drainage, wear, penetrations, and what the surface condition suggests.

Structure

How the house is carrying itself and where movement may be telling a story.

Systems

Electrical, mechanical, and visible performance issues that change decisions.

What Gets Noticed

A stronger site concept comes from inspecting the home, not decorating around it.

01

Site and drainage

Water movement is often the first silent clue.

02

Envelope and exterior

Siding, trim, openings, flashing, and how weather is being managed.

03

Attic, roof, and ventilation

The hidden spaces usually say more than the polished ones.

04

Interior systems and finish condition

Performance, wear, and the difference between cosmetic and consequential.
